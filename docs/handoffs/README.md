# Handoffs

Temporary successor-session handoffs belong here when local or in-flight state cannot be
recovered from current canon and the roadmap alone. Routine and documentation-only work
does not automatically need one.

Use the [session-handoff authoring guidance](../engineering/workflow.md#session-handoffs).
Name a handoff `YYYY-MM-DD-<workstream>.md`, identify its authoring snapshot and revision,
and check its claims against current repository state before acting. A handoff grants no
additional approval and does not override current canon.

After consumption, move useful current facts to their natural owners and historical evidence
to [research](../research/README.md). Retire the handoff when the follow-up ships or a newer
handoff supersedes it, after preserving unique useful information. Current status belongs
in [ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md); ownership and relocation rules belong to the
[documentation policy](../engineering/documentation.md).
